## Полный запрос

Use case: game-texture. Asset type: a single square seamless PBR BASE COLOR / ALBEDO image for a large rocky alpine mountain massif in an attractive readable historical 4X strategy game. Full-bleed continuous rock surface only, viewed exactly perpendicular to the surface, orthographic macro material scan, no landscape or object silhouette. Weathered slate and granite, medium neutral charcoal gray and warm taupe-gray, subtle ochre mineral accents, readable irregular stone plates and long fractured geological striations with fine granular mineral detail. Semi-realistic art-directed rock, large simple forms with restrained fine detail, not cartoon outlines. Seamless tileable edges in both directions. Even diffuse illumination, flat albedo color without baked directional light, cast shadows, ambient occlusion or glossy highlights. Restrained contrast, average medium-gray stone, neither nearly black nor white. No snow or moss in this source image: those will be placed on the model by height and slope in Blender. No framing, grid, panels, text, letters, numbers, logos, scale bars, labels, borders, transparency or watermark. Highest detail square texture, ideally 2048 by 2048.
